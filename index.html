Privacy by design

Your text is not our data.

Analysis happens in your browser using readable JavaScript modules. Cloudflare Pages serves the static files and the signed three-day trial cookie. There is no database for your resumes, code, answers, or tool text. An optional free workspace can save only redacted notes if you choose to create one.

01

Local analysis

Scores, plans, and checks are computed on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored by the demo.

02

Redacted prompts

The optional LLM receives only computed highlights. Contact patterns are removed before a prompt is built.

03

No payment layer

No card field, no subscription, no key, no tracker, and no third-party chat button.
